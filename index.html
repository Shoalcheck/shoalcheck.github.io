The whole flow · one finding, end to end

From a finding to a fix your team ships.

  1. 01 · Read

    Every object. Every rule. One pass.

    Read-only, with get and list: 27 kinds read, 104 objects, 103 rules, 1,917 checks — each kept with the objects it looked at.

  2. 02 · Find

    A finding you can defend in review.

    Right-size over-provisioned requests on workers/webhook-dispatcher: $300 a month at list price, from a week of the cluster's own usage — an upper bound, and the finding says so, beside the evidence it read.

  3. 03 · Ticket

    Into Linear in one command. Never twice.

    shoalcheck ticket push files the finding with its evidence, its basis and its patch. A marker in the ticket stops a second push from filing it again.

  4. 04 · Your AI agent

    Hand the whole cluster to your AI agent.

    Point Claude, Cursor or any MCP client at shoalcheck-mcp. It answers from the reading — 141 findings, grouped by the team that owns each namespace — and cannot reach the cluster.

  5. 05 · Every team

    Every team gets its own tickets.

    The agent files each team's share where that team works: Linear, or Jira through Atlassian's own MCP server. The 43 findings no namespace label claims go to triage — not to nobody.

  6. 06 · Fix

    The fix comes with the finding.

    The reading's own patch: CPU 2000m → 840m, memory 8192Mi → 4992Mi. Your team reviews it and applies it; Shoalcheck writes nothing to your cluster.

  7. 07 · Read again

    Read again. Gone is counted, not assumed.

    We applied that patch to the sample and read it an hour later: 1 finding no longer in the set, 0 new, 0 not evaluated — and $1,659 a month identified became $1,360. A finding that disappears is counted as missing, never as dealt with.

  8. 08 · CI/CD

    Stop the merge that grants cluster-admin.

    In any pipeline, shoalcheck gate compares the pull request's cluster with your baseline and exits 2 when the policy fires — here on one new critical finding, named: Review cluster-admin grant on ServiceAccount payments/ledger-exporter.

  9. 09 · Money

    Every dollar, with its rate.

    $1,659 a month across 5 rules — dated list prices and a week of the cluster's own usage, each row with what it was computed from.

  10. 10 · Reach

    See exactly who can reach cluster-admin.

    Every binding traced from its subject to the role it grants: 7 grants, 3 of them critical.

  11. 11 · Before a change

    Know before you drain.

    shoalcheck preflight drain ip-10-0-1-a exits 2 and names the pod that stops it. Free on every plan.

The audit path issues get and list. A recorder wraps the only transport the binary has and counts every request it makes; the figure you see is that count. Secret and ConfigMap values, environment variables, logs and node shells are never read at all.

Questions

What people ask before they run it on production.

Does it install anything in my cluster?

No. It is a binary on your machine that talks to the Kubernetes API with your kubeconfig, issuing get and list. There is no agent, operator, CRD or webhook, and nothing to uninstall.

What access does it need?

Read access to the kinds it lists — shoalcheck audit --show-permissions prints them before it connects. With less, it still runs, and says which kinds it could not read and why. A refused read is never reported as a clean one.

Does any data leave my machine?

Nothing about your clusters. The binary talks to your API server and, when one is reachable, to Prometheus. The only other request is the update check — shoalcheck upgrade, or the desktop app once a day if you leave that switch on — which asks github.com for the newest release and carries nothing about a cluster.

Which clusters does it work with?

Any conformant Kubernetes cluster it can reach. Costs are priced from bundled list prices for EKS, AKS and GKE; on any other cluster a figure says “unpriced” rather than inventing a rate.

What counts as a cluster on my plan?

A distinct cluster, by its kube-system UID, read with a licence in any 30 days — counted on your machine. Reading the same cluster again costs nothing, and preflight is never counted.

How is the licence checked?

Offline. A key is a signed document naming your plan, its expiry and its cluster cap, and the binary verifies the signature without phoning home. Paste it into the app under Plan & licence, or run shoalcheck licence install.

What does the free plan include?

Preflight in full, and the size of any reading: how many problems, how serious, of what kind, what they would cost, and the five most serious kinds. Which objects, the evidence and the fix for each are what a licence adds. Nothing is saved, written or exported.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

The first releases are not yet notarized by Apple or code-signed for Windows. Each release publishes the archives' SHA-256 sums, signed with cosign, so you can check what you downloaded with cosign verify-blob and the public key beside the releases.
