Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (the website). Controller: Illia Bilous (Bilous Illia Oleksandrovych), individual entrepreneur (FOP) registered in Rivne, Ukraine; contact support@shoalcheck.com.

The software

Shoalcheck runs on your machine. Nothing about your cluster leaves it unless you send it, for example by pushing tickets to your own Jira or Linear. There is no account and no analytics. The only automatic outbound request is an update check to github.com (releases of Shoalcheck); it carries nothing about a cluster and can be switched off.

Activating a Pro licence is a request you make: shoalcheck activate <code>, or an activation code pasted into the desktop app, sends our shop (shop.shoalcheck.com) the activation code and the fingerprint of each cluster you activate it for, and nothing else. A fingerprint is a one-way hash of the cluster's kube-system namespace UID: neither the UID nor the cluster's name, address or contents can be read back from it. The command prints exactly what it sends before sending it. The shop returns a licence key, which is then checked on your machine, offline.

The website

shoalcheck.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics script. It is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare, which sits in front of it as a CDN and proxy. Both receive your IP address as any web host does: GitHub under its privacy statement, Cloudflare under its privacy policy. Cloudflare also keeps aggregate traffic statistics for the site, such as the number of visits and the countries they come from; those totals are all we see, and they name no visitor. The page keeps one display preference in your browser's local storage, never sent anywhere. The walkthrough film is YouTube's: nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play, and from then the player comes from youtube-nocookie.com under Google's privacy policy.

Purchase data we process

  • Email address
  • Plan
  • Cluster fingerprints (a one-way hash of the kube-system UID) for Pro bindings, sent either at checkout or when you activate
  • Activation codes, and which cluster fingerprints each is bound to
  • Transaction IDs
  • When you activate: the IP address of the request, used only to limit repeated attempts and kept only as a hash, for no more than two hours

Purpose: issue and email your licence key or activation code, bind it to your clusters, handle support and refunds (legal basis: contract); the hashed IP address of an activation, to protect activation from abuse (legal basis: legitimate interest). Retention, except for that hash: for as long as the licence is active, then up to six years for accounting and dispute records; support email for up to two years.

Processors

  • Paddle (merchant of record): collects payment details and billing data under its own privacy policy; we never see card numbers.
  • Cloudflare (D1 database, hosting of the sales Worker): stores the data above.
  • Resend: sends the key email.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion and objection, as applicable under your law (e.g. GDPR). Contact: support@shoalcheck.com (privacy questions). Transfers outside your region: our processors (Paddle, Cloudflare, Resend, Google Workspace) transfer data under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.
